contributors to the Global WordNet Association. Credit both Princeton WordNet
and the Open English WordNet team when redistributing derived definitions.

This ZIP DOES NOT include the WordNet XML archive, nor a corpus of WordNet
English explanations. The optional builder downloads/uses the source on the
user's computer and embeds matched definitions in their generated HTML.
